# Upstream attribution

The menu-bar application and local Codex usage client in this repository are derived from [tollenceld/codex-usage-status](https://github.com/tollenceld/codex-usage-status). The base revision is `d8438da30833eadb660cb35d632d6da665a6ba23` on the upstream `main` branch.

The original MIT license and notice are preserved in [LICENSE](LICENSE), and the upstream history is retained. This derivative adds or adapts:

- a hover card for available reset opportunities, quota reset times, and scheduled data refresh;
- a macOS helper that starts the quota app with ChatGPT and quits it after ChatGPT exits;
- ChatGPT-bundled Codex executable discovery and configurable app paths;
- tests and documentation for the added behavior.

The upstream project and its contributors are credited above. The names “ChatGPT”, “Codex”, and “OpenAI” refer to their respective products and owners; this project is unofficial and unaffiliated.
